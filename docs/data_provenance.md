# Data provenance

## Wilcox Solar Observatory source

The numerical analysis starts from the WSO filled photospheric synoptic maps for Carrington rotations 1642–2182, spanning 27 May 1976 to 23 September 2016. The authoritative file route is:

```text
http://wso.stanford.edu/synoptic/WSO.{CR}.F.txt
```

The observatory describes the map collection at <http://wso.stanford.edu/synopticl.html>, its data policy at <http://wso.stanford.edu/DataPolicy.html>, and the requested acknowledgement at <http://wso.stanford.edu/src/ack.html>.

The complete source responses, including their WSO headers and record structure, are not committed. `scripts/download_wso_maps.py` writes them to `.cache/wso/filled_synoptic_maps/`, checks that each file contains exactly the requested 72 × 30 filled map plus only the permitted next-rotation seam record, and records its URL, byte count, and SHA-256 digest. The SHA-256 digest of the conventional checksum manifest for all 541 files is:

```text
e6188ead532be235cdb70952591b5363273e1b04bd674f0bfdd7d847aea4ed46
```

The maps should be cited following Duvall et al. (1977), *Solar Physics* **54**, 353–362, [https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00150874](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00150874), together with the WSO acknowledgement requested by the observatory.

## Committed numerical inputs

`data/wso_energy_cr1642_2182.csv` contains the nine energy series used by the cycle analysis. Its SHA-256 digest is:

```text
777962c8070ab8b1f9dc991b4d80cdfe262209c23aa184c15b2b47fb0b716ba6
```

Each of its 541 rows contains the Carrington rotation, conventional rotation-start time, the paper-specific decimal-year coordinate, and nine magnetic-energy series: full disc, north and south hemispheres, and the 0–15, 15–30, and 30–45 degree bands in each hemisphere. Values are in squared-gauss units under the reproduced orthonormal spherical-harmonic convention. The analysis clock is kept distinct from the UTC timestamp and uses `year + (month + day/31 - 1)/12`.

The preparation is causal and reproducible:

1. Parse the requested rotation by its record labels and omit the next-rotation seam.
2. Reverse the stored axes to increasing longitude and latitude.
3. Convert line-of-sight microtesla to radial field and then to gauss.
4. Bilinearly remap onto 60 Gauss–Legendre latitude nodes and 120 longitudes.
5. Compute orthonormal complex spherical-harmonic coefficients.
6. For each paper-defined region, zero all latitude rows outside its inclusive mask, transform again, and sum modal energies for degrees 1–15. Positive-order power includes its conjugate negative-order contribution.

Adjacent 15-degree masks share one endpoint row because the paper-specific array slices use inclusive endpoints. This convention is explicitly tested and is required to reproduce the series.

The two remaining numerical inputs contain the data plotted in Figures 1 and 2:

```text
05101315ce4bd1b1c052b5076f159bab6b04c24949a315c2dd00a6732da5a11b  data/wso_figure1_maps_cr1917_2149.csv
ae9dd5a13047a489b5dd04ab919ee4a8614d0fbdd6d03b43287c64b3b9cc3245  data/wso_mean_spectrum_l01_60.csv
```

`data/wso_figure1_maps_cr1917_2149.csv` contains the native 72 × 30 filled-map values and coordinates for Carrington rotations 2149 and 1917. `data/wso_mean_spectrum_l01_60.csv` contains, for each degree 1–60, the energy summed over all azimuthal orders and then averaged over Carrington rotations 1642–2182. Both files are regenerated by:

```bash
python3 scripts/prepare_figure_data.py
```

The pipeline plots these numerical values directly. No publication artwork is stored or used as plotting data.
